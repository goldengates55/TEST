Occupation data: NOC 2021 v1.0 (built in)

Built in: ESCO v1.2.1 in English and French (the language follows your job posting or resume), loaded when you choose ESCO, and NOC 2021 Version 1.0 for Canada (official, English). To use a newer NOC version, load your own CSV with columns code,title,teer,duties,examples. To use ESCO in another language or a newer version, load the ESCO CSV package (.zip) from esco.ec.europa.eu; the page remembers it in this browser. Verify every code.

US: O*NET-SOC (O*NET 31.0) is built in. UK: SOC 2020 is built in (unit group descriptions plus the coding index of about 32,000 job titles), loaded when you choose SOC 2020. Australia: OSCA 2024 v1.0 is built in the same way. New Zealand: NOL V3.0 is built in as interim data (codes and titles with the V2 and V3 changes applied, and no official descriptions yet). To replace it, choose NOL and load the full official file from Stats NZ. ANZSCO has been replaced in both countries.

Custom synonyms

Built-in synonyms are always on (for example ML, machine learning; JS, JavaScript; k8s, Kubernetes). Add your own, one group per line: ML = machine learning = deep learning. The first term is the one shown in reports.

Resume builder

Build a clean, single-column, ATS-friendly resume and download it as PDF, DOCX or plain text. Use Import to start from the resume you analyzed above, then apply the suggestions by editing the text.

Get each person's permission before listing their contact details.

PDF export supports Latin characters only; use DOCX for other scripts.

Cover letter

Creates a draft from your Resume builder and the job posting on the Scan tab. It only uses facts from your resume, and it uses the template chosen in the builder. Read and edit it before sending.

Jobs and employers

Official government job sites and employer registers for the region you chose above. This page cannot search those sites for you, and it does not scrape them. The buttons open the official site in a new tab, with your job title and place filled in where the site supports it.

Search an employer register you downloaded

Download an official register from the links above, load it here, and search it on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Being on a register does not mean an employer has vacancies or will sponsor you, so always check the current job and the employer.

Release watch

This page cannot send push notifications or check the internet in the background. Instead, Release watch tells you each time you open the site which classifications are due for a check, and a banner appears on the Scan tab when one is. Expected dates come from the official sites when this page was built, so confirm them on the source page.

When a new release is out: open the official page, download the files, then either load them with the Load button in the Occupation data box on the Scan tab (this replaces the built-in data in your browser; an ESCO ZIP is remembered), or send the files to the support team to rebuild the built-in data. Then press "I checked" below. For real alerts, also subscribe to each agency's own email or release calendar.

Suggestions need your confirmation. This tool never raises a degree level or adds a credential the resume doesn't state. Foreign credentials are flagged as possibly needing an Educational Credential Assessment (ECA). NOC 2026 is due in December 2026; codes here are NOC 2021 v1.0.
